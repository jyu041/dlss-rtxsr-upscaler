# Binary Distribution Boundary

The compiled project-authored worker may be distributed only as part of the
NVIDIA Video Enhancer application package with material additional
functionality. NVIDIA SDK portions are not relicensed under MIT and the SDK is
not distributed as a stand-alone product.

The package must include `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, retain applicable NVIDIA
notices, identify C55 by SHA-256, and keep the community `version.dll`, the
official NVIDIA feature/runtime files, and driver `nvofapi64.dll` external.
Applicable NVIDIA terms may require protective downstream terms, attribution,
mark handling, export compliance, and notification before commercial release.
This is not an invented EULA.
